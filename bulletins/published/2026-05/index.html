Key Developments

MMRC 2 announced with MUM-T ranges; Steckler names the institutional failure modes SAF must avoid

MINDEF announced MMRC 2 on 27 February 2026, operational by 2031, with two double-height ranges purpose-built for manned-unmanned teaming training. In May 2026, Jake Steckler in War on the Rocks documented the US Army's decade-long failed effort to integrate Apaches with Shadow drones and named the failure modes as cultural inertia, talent-model mismatch, and feedback suppression - not hardware.

SAF has roughly five years to write MUM-T training doctrine, draft the operator pathway, and design the curriculum that MMRC 2 will use. The Steckler failure modes are the warning. The right next action is a TDDG cross-branch working group with SAFTI MI and Army CSC to draft the MMRC 2 training-doctrine specification by end FY27, two years before facility delivery.

Project Flytrap 5.0 is the exercise SAFTI City was instrumented for

US 2nd Cavalry Regiment ran Project Flytrap 5.0 at Pabradė in Lithuania in May 2026, integrating over 20 pieces of counter-UAS and friendly-UAS equipment at troop and squadron level. The unit developed new TTPs for synchronising intelligence, fires, and maneuver under a multi-layered drone threat.

SAFTI City has 11,000 sensors and the capacity for two concurrent battalion-level exercises. The same mixed-stack drone problem could be instrumented at SAFTI City in ways the US 2nd Cavalry could not. Cap Dev (Sim) and the SAFTI City management team should co-design a SAF Flytrap-equivalent for the next training cycle, with Training Analytics owning the data product.

SOCOM generative-AI use sets the reference pattern for SAF training-admin AI

At SOF Week 2026 in Tampa, SOCOM disclosed heavy current use of generative AI for resource allocation and force deployment. The target is smaller models running at the tactical edge under fog-computing architectures, sourced from smaller startups rather than incumbent vendors.

The SAF Training Analytics branch is chartered to apply AI to training admin, safety, and outcomes. SOCOM has done the discovery and named the constraints. SAF can write a 90-day specification for the highest-value workflows (range booking, safety scans, exercise design assistance, outcome summarisation) and pilot one with a willing formation - rather than re-derive the same conclusions over 18-24 months.

Continuous Transformation acquisition model tested at division scale (Ivy Mass, May 2026)

The US 4th Infantry Division's May Ivy Mass event puts the next-generation command-and-control (NGC2) prototype against a red team for the first time at division scale. The Continuous Transformation acquisition model has over a dozen vendors building the system while soldiers test it. NGC2 was tested in DDIL conditions in Ivy Sting 4.

The Continuous Transformation model is the transferable lesson for SAF. SAF C2 programmes face the same question: build linearly with risk of obsolescence, or build-while-using with risk of incoherence. Doctrine Development should draft a short note on what concurrent build-and-train means for SAF acquisition doctrine. DDIL should be a configurable parameter at SAFTI City.
