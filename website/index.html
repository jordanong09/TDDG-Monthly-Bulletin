The conventional force is one institutional cycle behind unit-led drone integration and SOF-led AI adoption. Two May 2026 exercises (Project Flytrap 5.0, Ivy Mass) are the test of whether TDDG can close that gap.

Four developments in May 2026 frame this bulletin. War on the Rocks names Army Aviation's failed decade of manned-unmanned teaming as a PME problem, not a procurement one. Project Flytrap 5.0 at Pabradė shows 2nd Cavalry Regiment generating drone-warfare TTPs faster than TRADOC doctrine can absorb them. SOF Week revealed special operators are already using generative AI heavily for staff work while conventional formations have neither tools nor training. The Ivy Mass red-team event in May tests whether the 4th Infantry Division's next-gen command-and-control prototype can survive contact at division scale - a result that cascades into every CTC rotation, simulator, and training management system. The pattern across all four is the same: units and SOF are moving faster than TDDG's institutional cycle. Closing that gap is the work of the next quarter, not the next year.
