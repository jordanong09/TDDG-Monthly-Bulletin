Two May 2026 themes converge: AI is now reshaping how training itself is designed, and training must teach soldiers and leaders to use AI within the law of war. Both land at the moment SAFTI City is operational and MMRC 2 is on the books.

Nine developments across nine outlets cluster on two shifts the six TDDG branches own. First, AI is moving from a topic of training into the substrate of training - NATO ACT's Audacious Training, RAND's AI-warfare synthesis, and Breaking Defense's Army cyber wargame all describe AI changing how exercises are designed, delivered, and adjudicated. Second, soldiers and commanders now need explicit competency in operating AI-enabled systems under the law of armed conflict - Lieber Institute, CSIS, and RAND all point to this as a curriculum problem. SAFTI City and MMRC 2 are the venues where both shifts will play out for the Army.
